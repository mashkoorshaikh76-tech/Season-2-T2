ℹ️ How to use

  1. Create a room and share the code.
  2. Host enters three captains and the player pool, then saves setup.
  3. Randomize player order and put a player up for auction.
  4. Captains select their name and bid; updates appear live.
  5. Host sells to the highest bidder or marks the player unsold.

Prototype warning: browser-side controls are not tamper-proof. Use only with trusted friends; do not use for real money or valuable prizes.
